## When To Use

Use this skill to find product pages on Planet Beauty (`planetbeauty.com` / `www.planetbeauty.com`) for beauty, haircare, skincare, and salon-brand items.

## Minimal Successful Path

1. Go directly to `https://www.planetbeauty.com/`.
2. Dismiss the email/SMS/newsletter popup if it appears; it blocks normal page interaction.
3. Click the site search control/open-search icon.
4. Search with the target brand + distinctive product words + size/model if available.
5. Inspect the product cards in the results; ignore malformed or messy search-heading text.
6. Open the best matching product result.
7. Verify the product page URL follows the durable pattern:
   - `https://www.planetbeauty.com/products/{product-handle}`
   - Some pages may append `?variant={variant_id}` after opening.

## Do Not Do

- Do not keep searching or browsing the non-`www` surface; `https://planetbeauty.com` redirects to `https://www.planetbeauty.com/`, so start on the `www` URL directly.
- Do not leave the signup/text-offer popup open; close it before using search or navigation.
- Do not rely on a prebuilt/generated site tool for this domain unless one is explicitly available later; direct browsing and site search are the working route.
- Do not treat search-result heading/template artifacts as failure; the actual product cards are usable.
- Do not assume a product URL without `?variant=` is wrong; product cards may link to the canonical product handle, and the product page may later add a variant query.
- Do not browse broad menus or collections first; site search is the shortest reliable path to candidate product pages.

## Product Discovery Shortcuts

- Use site search with compact, high-signal terms:
  - brand
  - product line/name
  - model number or tool size
  - bottle size, bundle/duo terms, or gender/version when relevant
- Search results may show a small product count and brand filter context; use those cues to confirm the search is narrowed correctly.
- For bundles/duos, open both the bundle result and any nearby standalone component result only if needed to distinguish the exact item.
- Product pages live under:
  - `/products/{product-handle}`

## Verification Hints

- On product pages, verify:
  - brand name
  - product title
  - size/model/version
  - bundle vs standalone component
  - variant selection if the URL includes `?variant=`
  - stock/sold-out status when relevant
- Treat sale/regular prices as supporting evidence only; prioritize title, brand, size/model, and product-page description.
